# Pianta della Rete

A Da Vinci / Imola-style interactive city plan of the network. It is deployed on centuries at `/srv/docker/compose/hosts/centuries/mappa/` (nginx container `mappa` plus sidecar `mappa-sync`) and is served at `mappa.sqrd.link`. Current network state: [[Network Reference]].

## Design decisions (2026-09-25)
- Metaphor: fortified city (the site, `la-rocca`). The ER605 `la-porta` is the citadel at the centre, VLANs are walled wards (angle scales with host count), the ACL is walls plus red-chalk roads, Pangolin is the watchtower at the gate, and Tailscale is a tunnel under the walls.
- Not vendor-locked. It's a static site with no CDN, fonts are self-hosted (OFL), and there's no claude.ai dependency.
- Two data files:
  - `site/data/policy.json`, hand/agent maintained: VLANs, ACL in ER605 order, DNS, edge, Tailscale, aliases, `expect` assertions, `naming`.
  - `site/data/inventory.json`, generated by `tools/sync.py` from NetBox (required), Proxmox, Omada, and the_codex directory listing.
- Drift between NetBox and the policy is shown as "Errata" on the map and is never silently fixed.
- Agents follow `AGENTS.md`: never edit the SVG/JS or inventory.json, and run `tools/validate.py` after every edit.
- `no_ansible` on grimoire and nastradamus is intentional. The Ansible playbooks are for Linux servers only, and Proxmox/TrueNAS must not be updated by them. This is recorded in `policy.expect.no_ansible`.

## Deployment notes (2026-09-26)
- The sidecar talks to NetBox directly at `NETBOX_URL=http://netbox:8080` on the Docker `proxy` network, not through Pangolin. Through Pangolin, NetBox sees `10.10.100.252` as the source IP for everything.
- The NetBox user `mappa` has a view-only token (v2, `nbt_…`, sent as `Bearer`). Its Allowed IPs are `172.18.0.0/16` **and** `::ffff:172.18.0.0/112`. NetBox listens dual-stack, so IPv4 clients show up as IPv4-mapped IPv6 addresses.
- The object permission "mappa read-only" (view) covers dcim.device, dcim.interface, virtualization.virtualmachine, virtualization.vminterface, ipam.ipaddress and ipam.vlan. NetBox 4.x users can see nothing without an explicit permission.
- `.env` holds the token only, without a `Bearer ` prefix (`sync.py` strips one anyway). After changing `.env`, run `docker compose up -d --force-recreate`, because a restart doesn't reload `env_file`.
- `OMADA_SITE` takes the Omada siteId (survives renames) or the site name; empty means "the only site".
- First successful sync: 14 hosts, 4 drift notes.

## n8n NetBox sync workflows
- **Omada - NetBox Device Sync** (hourly). It syncs devices, VLANs, prefixes and gateway IPs, and since 2026-09-26 also the Omada management IPs into IP objects and `primary_ip4` for switches and APs. It matches devices on MAC and picks the primary Omada site, so renames are safe.
- **Proxmox - Netbox sync** (every minute). It syncs name, status, resources and tags only, not IPs. Its URLs are https (2026-09-26). It matches on **name**, so a rename in Proxmox becomes a delete plus a create. Rename in NetBox first until it matches on VMID.
- **Arcane - NetBox Container Sync.** It syncs containers as Services.
- nastradamus (TrueNAS) and pbs (TrueNAS VM, cluster `truenas`) have no sync source.
