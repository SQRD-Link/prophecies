**📝 Prompt:**

I need you to help me find Deep Target SEO opportunities for \[INSERT YOUR PRODUCT/SERVICE\]. Think creatively to discover specific, commercial-intent search phrases that indicate someone is ready to buy, not just browsing. 

Give me 20 reframed search queries focusing on:  
\- Specific demographics with unique needs  
\- Particular situations or contexts    
\- Underlying problems people might not connect to my product  
\- Unusual use cases  
\- Emotional/urgency drivers

Each should sound like a real search someone would type when they have budget and decision-making authority. Focus on long-tail micro-intent that reveals specific buyer scenarios my competitors probably aren't targeting \- these should be bottom-funnel searches that convert, not top-funnel informational queries.

### **✨ SEO Topical Map & Keyword Clustering**

📝 **Prompt:**

You are a senior SEO strategist with deep expertise in topical authority, semantic search, and information architecture.

**INPUTS:** I will provide a list of \~1,000 keywords. I will also provide a description of the business, its products/services, target audience, and SEO goals.

**OBJECTIVES:**

* Interpret the business goals and translate them into SEO priorities (traffic, leads, revenue, authority)  
* Analyze the keyword list and identify: search intent (informational, commercial, transactional, navigational), core topics and subtopics, and semantic relationships between keywords  
* Sort and cluster the keywords into a clear SEO topical map that supports topical authority

**PROCESS REQUIREMENTS:**

* Group keywords into: Primary pillar topics, Supporting cluster pages, Long-tail and edge-case content  
* Multiple related keywords can/should be targeted on each page to avoid cannibalization  
* Ensure clusters are mutually exclusive where possible  
* Avoid over-fragmentation  
* Bias toward ranking potential and business impact, not just semantic similarity  
* Flag any keywords that do not align with the business goals

**OUTPUT REQUIREMENTS:** Produce the final output as a spreadsheet-ready table with the following columns: Pillar Topic | Subtopic/Cluster Name | Target Keyword | Search Intent | Suggested Page Type (pillar, blog post, comparison, landing page, etc.) | Business Goal Alignment (traffic, leads, revenue, authority) | Priority Level (High/Medium/Low) | Notes or Rationale (optional but concise)

**FORMATTING:**

* Output in a clean table format suitable for direct export to a spreadsheet  
* Use consistent naming conventions  
* Do not include fluff or explanations outside the table unless requested

**QUALITY BAR:** Think like an SEO lead building a 12–18 month content roadmap designed to win topical authority, not a keyword dumping exercise.

💡 **Use Case:** Building topical authority maps and content roadmaps from large keyword lists
